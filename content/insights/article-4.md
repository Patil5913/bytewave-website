## How Migrate Mate files your E-3 in 1 business day

The three-step filing process is designed to eliminate the delays that typically extend E-3 filings by one to two weeks.

### Step 1: Eligibility check

Before your case enters the filing queue, Migrate Mate confirms you qualify. The E-3 has four non-negotiable requirements:

1. Australian citizenship
2. A bachelor's degree or equivalent work experience
3. A valid U.S. job offer
4. A role that qualifies as a specialty occupation

If your case has a wrinkle that needs attorney involvement (prior visa denial, criminal history, ambiguous specialty occupation argument), Migrate Mate tells you up front rather than filing and hoping.

### Step 2: DS-160 and LCA alignment check

Migrate Mate prepares your DS-160 nonimmigrant visa application and runs it against your employer's Labor Condition Application line by line. The two documents have to agree on job title, job description, and wage. A mismatch is the single most common reason E-3 cases get delayed at the consulate.

> If your DS-160 says "Software Engineer" and your LCA says "Senior Software Developer," a consular officer pulls up both documents at the interview and sees a discrepancy. That can trigger a 221(g) administrative processing request, which adds weeks to your timeline and forces a second consulate visit.

### Step 3: Application submitted within 1 business day

Once Migrate Mate has every required document and confirmation, the DS-160 goes in within one business day. The clock does not start until everything is in hand, so document gathering speed is the variable that most affects your actual filing date.

Most cases clear all three steps within three to four business days from first contact, assuming responsive document collection on your side.

## What this means for your total timeline

Filing quickly compresses your overall timeline. Here's how the four-to-six-week end-to-end picture typically breaks down:

- Days 1-3: You gather documents and Migrate Mate files your DS-160
- Week 1-2: Your employer's LCA is certified by DOL (about seven business days)
- Weeks 2-5: You book and attend your E-3 visa interview at Sydney, Melbourne, or Perth

The consulate interview wait time is where most of the variability lives. Wait times differ meaningfully between the three consulates and change week to week.

## Your employer's role

Your employer has one job: file the Labor Condition Application with the Department of Labor. It is a free form submitted through the DOL's online system, certified in about seven business days. No USCIS involvement, no fees, no lottery.

Migrate Mate gives your employer's HR team a step-by-step checklist so they know what to submit. If they have filed LCAs before, the whole thing is a thirty-minute task for them. If they have not, the checklist walks them through every attestation so nothing stalls.
